Kitsune is an unofficial Android app for Kitsu.app (Kitsu.io). It allows you to browse anime and manga, track your progress, and connect with other users on Kitsu.

Features:
* Explore and search anime and manga, even without an account
* View anime and manga details, including episodes/chapters and characters
* Manage your anime and manga library
* Scroll through feeds, join groups, and react to posts
* View other user profiles
* Update your Kitsu profile and account settings
* Home screen widget
